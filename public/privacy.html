Privacy Policy

Effective 9 September 2026

Gurthu Chey is a private medicine-reminder service operated by Aakash Narukula. It uses the minimum information needed to connect a family member to their schedule and deliver reminders.

Information used

  • Your verified mobile number for sign-in and account matching.
  • The member name, medicine names, days, and reminder times entered by the admin or member.
  • Basic device details and a Firebase messaging token to deliver schedule updates.

How it is used

Information is used only to authenticate users, sync the correct medicine schedule, generate reminder audio, and deliver schedule changes. Reminder calls and cached schedules continue locally when the phone is offline.

Service providers

Firebase provides authentication, database, messaging, and cloud functions. Truecaller may verify a phone number when the user chooses Truecaller login. Gurthu Chey requests only identity and phone-number scopes needed for login.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising. Data is visible only to the family admin and the member assigned to that mobile number. Data is retained until the admin deletes the member or the service is discontinued.

Contact

For access or deletion requests, email mail@gyftalala.com.
